Pick a slide to triage

Choose a whole-slide image on the left (a file such as .svs, .ndpi or .tiff, or a DICOM series folder) and press Triage. The app finds the tissue, embeds each tissue tile with the distilled student, and the triage model gives a call in WHO CNS5 terms. It is either a confident call, or the case goes to the pathologist to classify, with the reasons, and a map of where the model looked.

Nothing leaves this computer: the app listens on 127.0.0.1 only.
